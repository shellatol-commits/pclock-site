Alarm States

Understand the sequence so you know what to expect when the alarm is triggered.

1. Grace period — 15 seconds

A short grace period runs before the restrictive behavior starts.

  • Countdown is visible in the alarm window.
  • The grace period ends after 15 seconds.

2. After the grace period

When the grace period expires, the more restrictive alarm behavior begins.

  • The mouse becomes trapped.
  • Red warning text appears.
  • The alarm window repeatedly forces itself to the foreground.
